## 🎬 Recording & Production Checklist

1. **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60 FPS or clean 30 FPS.
2. **Audio:** Clear microphone voiceover without background noise.
3. **Cursor:** Smooth movement with mouse highlight enabled.
4. **Browser:** Chrome full screen (F11), dark mode enabled.
5. **Pre-load:** Login to dashboard beforehand so no auth delay occurs during recording.
6. **URL overlay:** Add text caption with live deployed URL during the closing 5 seconds.
